Service Design · Strategy

Krishi
Mitra

In the face of climate uncertainties, Krishi Mitra ensures timely labor access through phone or local offices. Krishi Mitra goes further by offering farm equipment and machinery rentals.

Type Service Design
Focus UI/UX, Strategy
Context Digital Impact Square (TCS)
Team MOVIS

The Challenge

Empowering Farmers
Through Technology

Indian farmers face mounting uncertainty — unpredictable weather, labour shortages, and inaccessible machinery. Krishi Mitra was designed to bridge these gaps through a service that meets farmers where they are.

  • 01 Labour shortages during critical harvest and sowing windows due to migration patterns
  • 02 High cost of owning specialised farm machinery — unaffordable for small landholders
  • 03 Limited digital literacy requiring both app and offline (phone/office) access channels
  • 04 Climate unpredictability making advance planning and real-time response critical

Research & Identity

Rooted in Context

Field research with farming communities in rural India shaped every design decision — from the multilingual interface to the offline-first architecture.

Service Design

How it works

01

Labour Access

Farmers request seasonal or daily labour via app, phone call, or local Krishi Mitra office. Workers are matched based on proximity, skill, and availability.

02

Machinery Rental

Tractors, harvesters, and specialised tools available for short-term rental. Booking through app or offline channels, delivered to the field.

03

Multi-Channel Access

Designed for low-literacy users — voice support, regional language toggles, and local office fallback ensure no farmer is left behind.
