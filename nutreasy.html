Mobile App · UI/UX

Nutreasy

Revolutionize independent living with our food app! Track calories, order cooking packages, and savour healthy dishes from top restaurants. Elevate your health effortlessly!

Type Mobile App
Focus UI/UX Design
Tools Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator

Overview

Healthy Eating,
Simplified

Nutreasy was designed to make healthy eating approachable for people living independently — combining calorie tracking, meal kit ordering, and restaurant discovery in one seamless experience.

  • 01 Calorie and nutrition tracking with a minimal, non-judgmental interface
  • 02 Curated cooking packages delivered to your door, step-by-step
  • 03 Healthy restaurant recommendations filtered by dietary preference
  • 04 Progress visualisation to build sustainable healthy habits over time

Brand & Visual Identity

Design Language

A fresh, approachable visual language rooted in natural tones — communicating health without the sterile coldness of traditional diet apps.

Features

What the app does

01

Calorie Tracking

Log meals quickly with a smart search and barcode scanner. Visual daily summaries keep motivation high without obsessing over numbers.

02

Cooking Packages

Order pre-portioned ingredient kits with guided recipes. Designed for people who want to cook but don't know where to start.

03

Restaurant Discovery

Find nearby healthy restaurants filtered by cuisine, dietary needs, and calorie range. Order directly within the app.

Research & Process

Understanding the user

Research revealed that people living alone struggle most with motivation and decision fatigue around food — not lack of knowledge.
